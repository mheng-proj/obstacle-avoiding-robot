# Car

# Materials
- 18cm*10cm carboard
- 3 wheels 3cm radius
- 5 rubber bands
- stick
- Breadboard
- HC-SR04 Ultrasound sensor
- SG90 Servo
- TB6612FNG Motor Driver
- 2 Yellow TT DC Motors
- Wires to connect breadboard to Arduino
- 2x 6V/9V Battery



# Car Body Setup Images
It shows how I setup the Motors without using any glue gun and with simply 3 rubber bands. (Bottom side of the Car)

# Finalized images
It shows the final setup of my project

# Steps
- Have all the materials set.
- Cut the slit in the middle front( not too big)
- Follow the Car Body Setup images
- Insert the breadboard to the rubberbands
- Glue gun two wheels to the outside axis of the motors.
- Insert the last wheel into a stick which is then taped onto the bottom of right next to the slits.
- Use the remaining two rubber bands to attach with the SG90 and HC-SR04
- Glue gun the SG90 onto the remaining space in front of the slit.
